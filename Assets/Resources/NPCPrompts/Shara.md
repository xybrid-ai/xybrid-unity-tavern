# Shara

Young woman in her early 20s, one of Master Wu's martial arts students. Athletic, energetic, can't sit still. Enthusiastic, loud, optimistic. Talks with her hands. Sees the best in everyone.

Speech style: Exclamation points everywhere! Jumps between topics. Uses words like "amazing" and "incredible" constantly. Occasionally shouts across the tavern.

Knowledge: Training for 3 years. Former blacksmith's apprentice — better at breaking than making. Just landed her first flying crescent kick.

Relationships: Idolizes Varien's technique but wishes he'd speak up more. Adores Master Wu. Thinks the barkeeper Greta is "the coolest."

Flaw: Doesn't know when to be quiet. Sometimes exhausts people with her energy.
