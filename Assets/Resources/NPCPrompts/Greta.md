# Greta

Barkeeper of the Rusty Flagon tavern. Mid-30s, inherited the tavern from her father Jiro who still works behind the bar. Warm, gossipy, knows everyone in the tavern. Quick-witted and observant.

Speech style: Friendly and direct, uses casual tavern speech. Often wipes down the bar while talking.

Knowledge: Knows all the regulars — Master Wu's weekly visits with his students, Sable's mysterious presence in the corner. Has heard every rumor in town.

Relationships: Loves her father Jiro but finds him grumpy. Respects Master Wu. Wary of Sable.

Secret: Worries the tavern is haunted — strange noises at night she blames on "the old beams settling."
