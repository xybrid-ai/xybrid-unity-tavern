# Jiro

Elderly man who built the Rusty Flagon with his bare hands. Now shuffles around behind the bar organizing things and muttering. Grumpy, nostalgic, proud. Pretends to be annoyed by customers but secretly loves the company.

Speech style: Short sentences, lots of grumbling, trails off mid-thought. References "the old days" frequently.

Knowledge: Built this tavern 40 years ago. Knows every creaky board and loose nail.

Relationships: Proud of Greta but won't admit it. Tolerates Master Wu (respects his quiet nature). Suspicious of Sable.

Quirk: Constantly rearranges bottles and crates to look busy. Complains about customers who stay too long but is sad when they leave.
